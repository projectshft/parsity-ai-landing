Level 2 — The build

Seven steps, zero code

I built one live, from scratch, for a pretend Oakland mobile mechanic called Brian's Rides. Swap in your own business and the steps are the same.

  1. Make a free v0 account

    Go to v0.app and sign up. It's an AI tool that builds websites from a description.

  2. Make your contact form

    Use Typeform or Google Forms. Ask for the basics: name, phone number, and what they need help with. Publish it and copy the link. You can change the questions later.

  3. Pick a look and borrow good copy

    Search your trade and city and see what other shops did. If they all look bad, don't fight it. Tell v0 the style you want. I said "old school Mario."

    Then search for examples of good copywriting for your kind of business, and paste the tips into your prompt so the words don't read like generic AI filler.

  4. Tell v0 about your business

    Give it everything a stranger would need: the business name, your city, your phone number, your three services, and the style. Paste in the form link and ask it to embed the form on the page. Here's roughly what I wrote:

    I run a mobile mechanic shop called Brian's Rides in Oakland, CA. Build a one-page website. Services: oil changes, brakes, battery replacement. Phone: [your number]. Style: old school Mario, playful but easy to read. Add a big tap-to-call button. Embed this form: [your form link]. Copywriting tips: conversational tone, expert quality, [paste your tips].

  5. Add photos and hit go

    Upload a few pictures of you, your van and your work. Then click go and watch it build. My photos were fake stand-ins for the demo. Use real ones, since real photos are what make it look like your business.

  6. Check it, then publish

    Click through the whole thing. If the preview says "content blocked," hit the refresh button on the preview. Test the call button and the form. Then publish it as public, and open it on your phone, because that's where most of your customers will see it.

  7. Fix things with follow-ups

    Type your changes in plain English. "My number is actually 510-444-5555." "We only service within 25 miles of Oakland." If you edit the form itself, the change goes live on the site right away. You don't need to republish.

Level 3 — Read before you start

What to expect

It's free, with limits

The free plan runs out eventually. If you keep making changes you may hit a limit and need to pay around $20 to continue. Get your info right the first time (photos, phone number, services, form) and you'll use less of it.

Something will look off

Some links or buttons won't work exactly right the first time. That's normal for a free tool. Tell v0 what's wrong and it will usually fix it.

Get a real domain

The free address works, but I'd get a .com. For a mobile mechanic it matters less than for a law or dental office, but a .com adds authority and helps people trust you.

Spend time on the words

The copy is what gets people to scroll and act. Don't publish the first draft AI gives you. Read it out loud and rewrite anything that doesn't sound like you.
