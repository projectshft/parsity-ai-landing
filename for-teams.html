Questions we get

Read this before you book.

What does the free session actually cover?

We pick one thing your team wants to build and we build it. Usually that is a RAG pipeline over a document set you already have, or a small agent that touches one of your internal tools. Your engineers write code during the hour.

Live or async, which should we pick?

Live if your team can get an hour together. Async if you are spread across time zones or your engineers cannot block the same hour. Same material, same instructor, same code.

What happens after the free session?

We tell you what a full engagement looks like and what it costs. If you say no, nothing happens. There is no follow up sequence and nobody calls you again.

Can we use our own codebase and data?

Yes, and it works better that way. If your data cannot leave your environment, we work with sanitized samples or your engineers run everything locally.

How many engineers can join?

Bring the team. The session works best with somewhere between three and fifteen people. If you are larger than that, tell me on the call and we will figure out the format.
