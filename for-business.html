Is this really free?

Yes. No card, no call required, no contract. We make money training teams to build this stuff. The video is how you find out whether we're worth hiring for that.

What kind of processes work best?

Anything repetitive that touches documents, email, spreadsheets, or a support queue. If a person on your team is copying information from one place to another, that's usually a good candidate.

Do I need a technical team?

No. Some of the best submissions come from people who've never written a line of code. If the answer requires engineers you don't have, we'll say that in the video instead of pretending otherwise.

How long does it take?

A few business days. A person records these, so we'd rather be honest about the queue than promise you something in an hour.

Only if you ask. You get the video whether you reply or not, and there's no follow-up sequence attached to it.
