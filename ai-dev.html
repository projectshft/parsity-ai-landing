Frequently asked questions

Is this actually mentor-led, or is "mentor" doing some heavy lifting?

You get six live 1:1 sessions with a senior engineer, paired to your schedule, not a cohort's. Every project you submit gets written feedback from a person who has shipped this stuff in production, not an autograder and not a model telling you your code looks fine. That's what you're actually paying for.

Do I have to get on a call to sign up?

Yes, and it's the one part of this we don't skip. Fifteen minutes, no pitch, just making sure this is actually the right move before either of us spends the time or the money.

I already use Claude or Cursor every day. Is this for me?

Depends how you're using it. If you're pasting code between chats and hoping, reviewing every single line because you don't trust the output, or your team can't agree on what "good" looks like when a model writes the first draft, yes. If you already have a real system and you're shipping clean, this program won't do much for you.

What level do I need to be?

Any level, junior to senior. You need to be able to build and deploy apps. You don't need AI experience. You'll have it when you're done. The language you use doesn't matter, this isn't about syntax.

Will I turn into a "meat proxy" who just forwards prompts to Claude?

The opposite. The actual skill gap right now isn't typing speed, it's engineering judgment: knowing what's worth building, when to trust the output, and when to throw the whole thing out and start over. That's the entire curriculum.

How is this different from other programs?

Most programs teach you to call an API and call it AI engineering. We teach the architecture companies are actually hiring for: RAG from scratch, agent systems, LLM orchestration, vector databases, production patterns, and a human who reads your code every week.

Is this the "exclusive job opportunities" thing an employment guarantee?

No, and be suspicious of anyone who tells you they can guarantee you a job. What you get is a head start: leads through our network before they're posted everywhere else, plus interview prep and resume help so you're actually ready when one lands.

Why the communication module?

Engineers who can build and communicate are worth far more than engineers who can only build. Every developer who's landed an AI lead role out of this program says the same thing: being able to explain their work to leadership was the difference. Nobody else trains this. We do.

Yes. We provide invoices and training budget request templates. Many companies have L&D budgets specifically for upskilling engineers.
